CMR Perfusion Analyser

Decision-support / research tool. Not for standalone clinical diagnosis. Results depend on the thresholds in Settings, which you must check. Always interpret alongside the images, LGE, and the full clinical picture.
No patient identifiers. Do not photograph names, NHS numbers, dates of birth or other identifiers. Frame or crop the photo so that only the results table is visible. Photos are sent to the Claude API to read the numbers. This app does not save photos or values.
